## 1. LinkedIn Connected App: Activating Gamma

Gamma is an officially recognized **LinkedIn Connected App**. By connecting Gamma to your LinkedIn account, your platform activity and capabilities can be integrated into your professional profile.

### Step-by-Step Connection Instructions:
1. **Log in to Gamma**: Go to [gamma.app](https://gamma.app) and sign in.
2. **Access Account Settings**:
   - Click on your avatar/workspace name in the top left corner.
   - Navigate to **Settings** → **Connected Accounts** (or **Integrations**).
3. **Connect LinkedIn**:
   - Locate the **LinkedIn** tile.
   - Click **Connect** and authorize the OAuth permission request.
4. **Verify Connection in LinkedIn**:
   - In LinkedIn, navigate to: **Settings & Privacy** → **Data Privacy** → **Other applications** → **Permitted Services / Connected Apps**.
   - Confirm **Gamma.app** is listed as an active authorized connected application.
